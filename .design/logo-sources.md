# Framework and protocol logo sources

Retrieved 15 October 2026. Assets are bundled in `public/logos` so catalog browsing does not depend on a third-party image host. Marks identify their respective frameworks, companies, or protocols; they do not imply a partnership or endorsement.

- OpenAI, Claude, Google, LangGraph, LangChain, n8n, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, CrewAI, Microsoft, Pydantic AI, Mastra, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face and MCP: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), npm `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` version **1.95.1**. Monochrome `currentColor` values are resolved to the site's pearl color for standalone SVG images. The original MIT license is included at `public/logos/LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE`.
- ChatGPT dots uses its company mark, OpenAI; smolagents uses its company mark, Hugging Face.
- Agno: [official Agno documentation white wordmark](https://github.com/agno-agi/docs/blob/main/logo/white.svg).
- A2A: [official A2A white protocol icon](https://github.com/a2aproject/A2A/blob/main/docs/assets/a2a_logo/icon/white/SVG/a2a_icon_white.svg). Protocol reference: [A2A documentation](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/).

Images are decorative beside visible framework/protocol names and have empty alt text. Explicit dimensions reserve their space. All assets are checked for valid SVG markup and external resource dependencies.
