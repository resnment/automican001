- Confirmed content mismatch: existing 30-role software-delivery taxonomy is not a recognizable employee catalog for the requested audience. Replace it entirely with everyday business/institutional/personal roles.
- Confirmed inquiry mismatch: organization is required for every visitor. Add audience selection, allowing personal visitors to omit an organization.
- Aesthetic finding (taste, user requested): charcoal/mint flat cards underserve the requested classy sci-fi direction. Adopt ink-blue layered surfaces, pearl typography, champagne actions and restrained glacial cyan/lilac illumination.
- Preserve: email inquiry review/download, draft retention, app-owned dialogs, accessible validation, local assets, honest data claims and no fabricated pricing.
